# Domain Suites and Product-Style Tools

This page covers grouped tools and larger product-style subtrees inside `tools/utilities/`.

## Context_Analysis_Suite

- **Path**: `tools/utilities/Context_Analysis_Suite`
- **Why it matters**: bundles chat parsing and video analysis around contextual review workflows.
- **Potential fit**: intake-side suite for multimodal conversation review and artifact extraction.

## Voice_Analysis

- **Path**: `tools/utilities/Voice_Analysis`
- **Why it matters**: contains voice-oriented application assets including `Chronicle_Voice_App` and `story-voice-backend`.
- **Potential fit**: voice evidence review, transcript handling, or audio-derived narrative generation.

## conflict-analysis

- **Path**: `tools/utilities/conflict-analysis`
- **Why it matters**: appears to package legal or conflict-analysis workflows.
- **Potential fit**: adversarial narrative comparison, contradiction mapping, or custody/conflict evidence review.

## document-processing

- **Path**: `tools/utilities/document-processing`
- **Why it matters**: includes document conversion and authoring tools such as Pandoc-oriented assets.
- **Potential fit**: report generation, conversion pipelines, and export formatting.

## forensic

- **Path**: `tools/utilities/forensic`
- **Why it matters**: keeps forensic-data utilities close to the evidence domain.
- **Potential fit**: analyst UI, refinement, or evidence transformation workflows.

## ml-nlp

- **Path**: `tools/utilities/ml-nlp`
- **Why it matters**: collects ML and NLP-focused utilities like manipulation or toxicity analysis.
- **Potential fit**: classification sidecars, labeling references, and behavior-analysis experiments.

## story-voice

- **Path**: `tools/utilities/story-voice`
- **Why it matters**: likely connected to voice/narrative synthesis or story reconstruction.
- **Potential fit**: summarization or narrative retelling layers for reviewed evidence.
